Privacy Policy

Your calm space is also your safe space.

Last updated: January 2025

Welcome

At CalmNow, we believe your peace of mind should never come at the cost of your privacy. We've designed our mindfulness app to support your mental well-being while collecting only what's truly necessary.

This policy explains, in simple and clear terms, how we handle your information. No complex legal jargon—just honest, straightforward communication.

Information We Collect

We keep data collection minimal and purposeful:

  • Account information — Your email address and name when you sign up via email or Google login
  • Usage patterns — How you interact with the app, session lengths, and features you use most
  • Your preferences — Saved sound mixes, favorite meditations, and personalization settings (optional)

What we don't collect: We never intentionally collect sensitive personal data, health records, or any information beyond what's needed to provide you a calming experience.

How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us create a better, more peaceful experience:

  • Personalization — Remembering your favorite soundscapes and meditation preferences
  • App improvement — Understanding which features bring you the most calm
  • Performance — Ensuring the app runs smoothly and reliably
  • Communication — Sending important updates about your account (only when necessary)

Data Storage & Security

Your information is stored securely using industry-standard practices. We use trusted infrastructure providers and implement encryption to protect your data both in transit and at rest.

Our promise: We will never sell your personal data. Your trust is far more valuable to us than any data marketplace.

Third-Party Services

To provide you with a seamless experience, we work with these trusted partners:

Supabase Google Login Stripe

These services only receive the information necessary to perform their function. They are bound by their own privacy policies and security standards.

Your Rights

You're always in control of your information. You can:

  • Access your data — Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Update your information — Correct or modify your account details anytime
  • Request deletion — Ask us to remove your data from our systems

Simply reach out to us, and we'll respond promptly and respectfully.

Data Retention

We only keep your information for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days.

Some anonymized usage data may be retained for improving the app, but this cannot be linked back to you.

Children's Privacy

CalmNow is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

If we make meaningful changes to this privacy policy, we'll let you know through the app or via email. We believe in transparency and will always give you time to review any updates before they take effect.

Get in Touch

Have questions about your privacy? We're here to help. Reach out to us anytime—we respond within 48 hours.
